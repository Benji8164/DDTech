Recognizing a stroke

Spot the signs with BE-FAST

Stroke symptoms usually appear suddenly. BE-FAST is an easy way to remember the six main warning signs.

B

Balance

Sudden loss of balance or coordination, dizziness, or trouble walking.

E

Eyes

Sudden blurred or double vision, or loss of vision in one or both eyes.

F

Face

One side of the face droops or feels numb. Ask the person to smile.

A

Arm

One arm is weak or numb. Ask the person to raise both arms; one drifts down.

S

Speech

Slurred or strange speech, or trouble understanding. Ask the person to repeat a simple sentence.

T

Time

Note when symptoms began and go to the nearest hospital or emergency center right away. Every minute matters.

If you notice any of these signs, take the person to the nearest hospital or emergency center immediately.

Why are strokes so often missed?

Sudden onset

Symptoms can appear within seconds, with no earlier illness to warn you.

Signs go unnoticed

A drooping face or a weak arm is easy to mistake for tiredness, especially when nobody is around.

Elderly living alone

An increasing number of older people live alone, with no one nearby during the hours that matter most.

Family can’t be there 24/7

Busy schedules and distance leave elderly relatives without 24/7 supervision.

Peace of mind with four simple steps

  1. Wear

    The elderly wearer puts it on like any other watch. Nothing to tap or configure each day.

  2. Signs

    Motion sensors compare movement between the left and right arm. An algorithm looks for signs of one-sided weakness, one of the warning signs in the BE-FAST method, and raises an alert when something looks wrong.

  3. Alert

    Everyone you link gets an app and SMS alert with the wearer’s live location. The wearer can cancel a false alarm during the first 60 seconds.

  4. Support

    One press connects to our 24/7 call center, who help coordinate emergency response.

Questions families ask

Does the watch replace emergency services?

No. If you suspect a stroke, go to the nearest hospital or emergency center immediately. StrokeGuard helps alert your family and connect to our operators faster.

Does it need a smartphone?

No. The watch has its own 4G SIM and GPS, so it works on its own without a phone or the app. Family members use the StrokeGuard app on their phones to receive alerts and see location.

What if the watch gives a false alert?

When the watch detects a possible emergency, it warns the wearer first. If the wearer is fine, for example after bumping into something, they can cancel during the first 60 seconds. If it is not cancelled, the alert is sent to family and our call center.

How long does the battery last on one charge?

The battery lasts about one week on a single full charge.
