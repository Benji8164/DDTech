Why strokes are so often missed

Sudden onset

Symptoms can appear within seconds, with no earlier illness to warn you.

Signs go unnoticed

A drooping face or a weak arm is easy to mistake for tiredness, especially when nobody is around.

Parents living alone

More older people live alone, with no one nearby during the hours that matter most.

Family can’t be there 24/7

Work, distance and daily life make constant in-person care impossible.

Peace of mind, in four steps

  1. Wear it

    Your parent wears the watch like any other. Nothing to charge-check, tap or configure each day.

  2. It watches for early signs

    Motion sensors compare movement between the left and right arm. An AI model looks for signs of one-sided weakness, one of the warning signs in the BEFAST method, and raises an alert when something looks wrong.

  3. Family is alerted at once

    Everyone you link gets an app and SMS alert with the wearer’s live location.

  4. A real person picks up

    One press connects to our 24/7 call center, who help coordinate emergency response.

Questions families ask

Does the watch replace emergency services?

No. If you suspect a stroke, call your local emergency number (115 in Vietnam) immediately. An Tâm helps alert your family and connect to our operators faster.

Does it need a smartphone?

The watch has its own 4G SIM and GPS. Family members use the free app on their phones.

What if the watch gives a false alert?

The wearer can cancel an alert within seconds, and the family is told it was a false alarm.
